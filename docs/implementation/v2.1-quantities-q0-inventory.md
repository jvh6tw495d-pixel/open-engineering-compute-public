## 3. Physical-field audit

### Electrical

The six electrical skills enable both `validation.dimensional: true` and
`validation.physical: true`. Their fixed-unit inputs declare `x-oec-unit`.
Dimensionless fields such as power factor, efficiency, phase count and
per-unit magnitude correctly remain plain numbers.

`electrical.per_unit_conversion.value` has a unit selected by
`quantity_kind`, so a static `x-oec-unit` cannot represent it. ADR 0016
documents the skill-local validation and conversion exception. The exception
must remain explicit in the future audit rather than being silently ignored.

### Energy and battery

These are confirmed v2.1 audit gaps:

- `energy.balance`: energy arrays and `storage_delta` are bare numbers;
- `energy.load_metrics`: `power_values` is an array of bare numbers;
- `battery.soc_step`: `power`, `dt_hours` and `capacity` are bare numbers;
- all three manifests set `dimensional: false` and `physical: false`;
- `battery.soc_step/output.schema.json` is only
  `{"type": "object", "additionalProperties": true}`.

`soc`, efficiencies and tolerances may remain dimensionless, but the physical
power, time, capacity and energy values need explicit contracts. Therefore the
roadmap's phrase “zero bare float in physical skills” cannot pass while these
packages remain unchanged, even though Q6 names only electrical skills.

### Outputs

Electrical output schemas generally use explicit `{value, unit}` objects.
The existing dimensional validator and central normalization operate on
inputs. v2.1 must decide whether output unit enforcement is:

1. a schema-authoring gate only; or
2. a new result validator that checks quantity shape and canonical unit.

This decision must be made before claiming 100% physical I/O coverage.

## 4. Contract decisions required

1. **Public name:** keep `QuantityValue` as the stable JSON DTO, or introduce
   `Quantity` as an alias/wrapper. Do not create two competing serialized
   shapes.
2. **Dimension API:** define whether callers receive a stable OEC dimension
   representation or a Pint-specific object. Public contracts should not leak
   backend internals accidentally.
3. **Arithmetic scope:** specify supported operations, result-unit behavior
   and dimensional errors before implementing operators.
4. **Offset units:** explicitly cover Celsius/Fahrenheit conversions and
   prohibit ambiguous multiplicative operations.
5. **Original-unit provenance:** preserve the current `inputs` versus
   `normalized_inputs` semantics.
6. **Constants:** choose a small set, pin its authoritative source/version and
   record value, unit, precision and citation.
7. **Uncertainty:** v2.1 should introduce representation only (nominal value,
   absolute or relative uncertainty, kind/source). General propagation belongs
   to v2.3.
8. **Dynamic-unit fields:** formalize an auditable exception mechanism for
   fields whose unit depends on another input.
9. **Output enforcement:** choose schema gate versus runtime result validator.
10. **Compatibility:** `ExecutionResult`, REST and MCP shapes stay additive and
    unchanged through v2.1.

## 5. Automated gates

The v2.1 gate should be implemented as executable checks:

- every manifest with physical fields enables dimensional validation, unless
  it carries a documented machine-readable exception;
- every physical scalar/array input and output is a quantity shape or a
  documented dimensionless field;
- every `x-oec-unit` is parseable by the shared registry;
- every quantity schema requires exactly `value` and `unit`;
- round-trip, idempotence and incompatible-dimension properties cover
  multiplicative and offset units separately;
- dynamic-unit exceptions such as `per_unit_conversion.value` have dedicated
  validation and tests;
- all six electrical skills pass the authoring audit;
- energy and battery migrations pass before the global “zero bare float”
  claim;
- SDK, CLI, REST and MCP conformance remains green;
- full tests, Ruff, format, mypy, forbidden-names and installation smoke are
  green.

## 7. Explicit non-goals

- Math IR;
- general uncertainty propagation;
- tensor quantities;
- Physics Complete;
- breaking `ExecutionResult`;
- public release or remote push.

## 8. Q0 verdict

v2.1 is **PARTIAL, not started from zero**. Conversion and input
normalization are usable foundations. The blocking work is contract
stabilization plus enforceable physical I/O coverage, especially the currently
dimensionless-by-schema energy and battery packages.
